Switching View Types

How to change view types

Desktop

  1. If you have more than one split view open, click on the view you want to change to make sure it has focus (the focused view will be highlighted with a brighter header color)
  2. In the toolbar, click on the view name you wish to select

Note: If your window is small, the toolbar may show icons only. Hover over the icons to see the name of each view.

Tablet

  1. If you have more than one split view open, tap on the view you want to change to make sure it has focus (the focused view will be highlighted with a brighter header color)
  2. In the toolbar, tap on the view selector, and then tap the name of the view you want to see in the menu

Mobile

Portrait orientation:

  • Click on the Clock icon () in the bottom toolbar to view a Chronological list of items
  • Click on the Book icon () in the bottom toolbar to view items listed in Narrative order

Landscape orientation:

  • Click on the Clock icon () in the toolbar to open Timeline View
  • Click on the Book icon () in the toolbar to open Narrative View

How to open two different views

Desktop

  1. Split your workspace into two different views by clicking on the split icon () in the workspace header 
  2. Click into the top view to give it focus
  3. In the toolbar, click on the view name you want to open
  4. Click on the second view to give it focus
  5. In the toolbar, click on the view name you want to open

Tip: You can hold down the Alt/Option key to split the view horizontally instead

Tablet

  1. Tap on the split icon in the toolbar ( or )
  2. Tap on your preferred split layout in the menu
  3. Tap on the top view to give it focus
  4. In the toolbar, tap on the view selector, and then tap the name of the view you want to see in the menu
  5. Click on the second view to give it focus
  6. In the toolbar, tap on the view selector, and then tap the name of the view you want to see in the menu
